# Privacy

Squint works on images on your Mac. It never uploads, transmits or keeps a copy of your images, or of anything read from them, anywhere else. A file is changed only when you ask Squint to change it, and only in the way the chosen treatment describes.

## Mac App Store edition

The Mac App Store edition makes no network connections. It does not collect any data, and it contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or account code. The link at the foot of its window opens the releases page in your browser, and only when you click it.

## Edition on the releases page

This edition makes one kind of network request: checking for an update, through Sparkle. It asks once before it checks on a schedule, and the "Check for Updates" menu item checks when you choose it. A check fetches `appcast.xml` from raw.githubusercontent.com. Installing an update downloads the release archive from github.com. GitHub receives each request and your IP address, as it does for any download. The request's user agent names Squint and its version. Squint does not send an identifier of its own or any information about your Mac. GitHub's privacy statement describes what GitHub does with requests: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
